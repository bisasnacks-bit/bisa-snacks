Frequently asked questions

What is Kozhiada?

At BISA, we prepare Kozhiada the way it has always been meant to be made — slow-cooked with onions, black pepper, fennel, curry leaves, and special Malabar masalas. It is intentionally kept dry to retain its signature crispness for days.

What ingredients are used?

Our Kozhiada is made with chicken, flour, cocunut, onion, garam masala, chilli powder, salt, ginger, ricebran oil, turmeric powder, garlic, and curry leaves.

Does BISA use preservatives?

No. We do not add preservatives to artificially extend the shelf life of our Kozhiada.

How long does BISA Kozhiada stay fresh?

BISA Kozhiada stays fresh for up to 50 days when stored properly.

How should I store my Kozhiada?

Your Kozhiada comes with a permanent seal for freshness. To open the package, simply tear off the top section just above the temporary seal.

The temporary seal can then be used to reseal the package, helping protect your Kozhiada from moisture and air. For best results, we recommend transferring it to a dry, airtight jar and keeping it away from moisture.

Do you deliver outside Kerala?

Yes, we try to make BISA Kozhiada available across India, straight from our kitchen.

Delivery to your location depends on the availability of a delivery partner in your area. Send us your PIN code and we’ll let you know if delivery is available.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery charges depend on the delivery address and are calculated accordingly.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is generally expected within 4–5 days, although most of our packages reach customers within 2–3 days.

Can I place a bulk order?

Absolutely! BISA Kozhiada can be a delicious addition to your parties and gatherings.

We also offer discounted prices for bulk orders. Please reach out to us for bulk-order enquiries.

Can I order Kozhiada as a gift?

Premium BISA Kozhiada gifting packages are coming soon. Stay tuned!
